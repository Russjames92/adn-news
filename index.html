Opinion

About ADN News

ADN News — Any Day Now — is a satirical Christian news outlet covering the end times with the seriousness they deserve. We are recovering dispensationalists, postmillennial optimists, and firm believers that the Kingdom of God is advancing — whether or not a blood moon appeared last Tuesday.

We poke fun at sensational eschatology because we've lived it, preached it, and eventually had to repent of it. Our satire is offered in love — the same love that compels us to say: the gospel is winning, Christ is reigning, and the Great Commission was not given to a people who expected to fail.

"The earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the Lord as the waters cover the sea." — Habakkuk 2:14

All stories are entirely fictional. Any resemblance to actual prophecy conferences, countdown clocks, or blood moon festivals is purely coincidental and frankly inevitable.
