Local Prophecy Conference Postponed After Host Realizes 'This Generation' Has Now Passed Three Times

Organizers of the 2026 "Final Hours" Summit announced a "brief indefinite delay" after keynote speaker Rev. Hal Endtimes, Jr., discovered a significant timeline problem.

Chester T. Rapture Senior Eschatology Correspondent · May 26, 2026 · 4 min read Satire 100%

TULSA, OK — The 2026 "Final Hours Prophecy Summit," billed as "the most urgent prophecy conference since the last eleven," has been postponed indefinitely after organizer and keynote speaker Rev. Hal Endtimes, Jr. discovered Sunday morning that the generation Jesus referenced in Matthew 24:34 has technically expired on their published timeline not once, not twice, but on three separate occasions since 1948.

"We just need a few more weeks to recalibrate," Rev. Endtimes told ADN News by phone, audibly flipping through a Scofield Reference Bible. "The issue is we defined 'this generation' as 40 years in 1988, then as 70 years in 2018, and then as 80 years last spring. We're running out of Psalm 90 options."

"The good news is that prophetic urgency has never been higher. The bad news is it's been this high since 1967."
— Rev. Hal Endtimes, Jr.

Vendors who had already shipped merchandise — including 4,000 copies of Rev. Endtimes' new book Any Day Now: Why This Time Is Different (Revised Edition) — were told to hold inventory "pending clarification." Pre-orders for the conference's exclusive "Rapture Ready Resource Kit," which retails for $89.99 and includes a laminated prophecy chart, a blood-moon tracking journal, and a bumper sticker reading "My Other Car Was Raptured," are reportedly still processing.

The Generation Problem

Biblical scholars have long noted the interpretive challenges of the Olivet Discourse, but few have experienced those challenges quite so viscerally as Rev. Endtimes, who has staked three separate ministry rebranding campaigns on the "terminal generation" concept.

"In 1988, the consensus was clear: 1948 plus 40 years," he explained. "When that passed, we went to 70 years — a generation in Psalm 90. That was 2018. When that passed, we recalibrated to 80 years, which gives us until 2028. Honestly, if we get to 2028 we may need to revisit whether 'generation' could mean something more like a 'season' or possibly an 'epoch.'"

ADN Editorial Note: The Greek word genea in Matthew 24:34 most naturally refers to the generation of people Jesus was actually speaking to — which would place the fulfillment of those events in the first century, prior to 70 AD. This is the position of partial preterists and is held by theologians including R.C. Sproul, who called it "the most embarrassing verse in the Bible" for those holding a futurist view. Just a thought.

Conference attendees who had already booked non-refundable flights to Tulsa were offered a "prophetic rain check" — a certificate redeemable at any future Final Hours Summit, valid "until the actual Final Hours, whichever comes first."

The Broader Industry Responds

The postponement has sent minor ripples through the prophecy conference circuit. Three competing events — "Midnight Hour Ministries Annual Convocation," "Signs of the Times 2026," and the perennial "It's Definitely Now Weekend Retreat" — have released statements distancing themselves from any timeline calculations associated with 1948 and are reportedly exploring 1967 as a fresh starting point.

"We feel very confident about 1967," said one organizer who asked to remain anonymous. "The six-day war is a clean, defensible baseline. That gives us until 2037 on the 70-year model, which is plenty of runway to sell conference tickets and produce content."

Asked for comment, a spokesperson for the broader postmillennial community simply forwarded a quote from Habakkuk 2:14 and did not respond further.
